Vitals for macOS · Share · clipboard and files between Mac and phone

Share, on the Mac.

Your paired phones and the Mac trade files both ways, LocalSend style, over the Wi-Fi or Tailscale route Remote already uses. The phone's latest copy shows up in the menu bar, and ⌃⌥⌘V sends the Mac's clipboard to the phone. Nothing new is always on screen: the menu bar panel grows a Share section only while something is happening, the menu bar and Dock icons take drops, Finder gets a Quick Action, and Remote gets a Sharing segment. Everything on the desktop below is clickable and draggable. Or run a journey.

What changes

WhereChange
Menu bar iconTakes file drops. While a transfer runs it shows the percent next to the glyph. Otherwise unchanged.
Menu bar panelNew Share section at the top, only while something is happening: asks, transfers with pause and cancel, waits, the last finished drop, and the phone's latest copy with Copy. The actions row gains Send clipboard, labelled with its shortcut.
Dock iconTakes file drops and opens Remote › Sharing. Progress bar on the tile while sending.
FinderQuick Actions › Send to Phone, plus Services › Send to Phone with Vitals as the fallback. Both open the phone picker when more than one phone can get files.
Phone picker"Send to Pixel 9 Pro or iPhone 17?" Checkboxes, last used phone checked, offline phones still listed (they wait). Skipped when only one phone can get files.
Remote › DevicesEach phone gets a Files checkbox next to Read clipboard. The Clipboard card moves out.
Remote › SharingNew segment between Devices and Activity: Files (save folder, auto-accept, web ask, largest drop, Send Files…), Recent, and the Clipboard card with two new rows.
SettingsShortcuts: Send clipboard to phone, default ⌃⌥⌘V, recordable, plus who it goes to.
NotificationsGot, Wants to send (Accept, Decline), Sent, Waiting, Clipboard sent. No banner while the panel is open, since the panel already shows it.
Received filesSaved to ~/Downloads/Vitals. DMG, PKG and app files keep the quarantine flag, so Gatekeeper checks them before they run.
