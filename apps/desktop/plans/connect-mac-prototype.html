Connect, on the Mac.

Open a phone from the new Devices page and get its photos, videos and shared folders onto the Mac. Or turn on Show phones in Finder and open the phone like a drive. Phones only see the Mac folders you list in Settings, and a terminal stays off until you allow it for that phone. When a phone is browsing or has a terminal open, the menu bar shows it, with Stop and End right there. Everything below is clickable, or run a journey. Phone side: phone prototype. What changes

WhereChange
SidebarNew Connect group with Devices. Remote moves under it. One nav level: a phone opens inline with a Devices back link, never a tab.
DevicesPaired phones and browsers with route and state. An online phone opens to its Places (Photos, Videos, Downloads › Vitals, folders shared on the phone). A phone in the background says to open Vitals on it. Get and Get All pull through Drop into ~/Downloads/Vitals.
Quick LookClick a thumbnail for a big preview, fetched from the phone at screen size, not the full file.
FinderSettings › Remote › Show phones in Finder mounts each online phone under Locations. Vitals serves it as WebDAV on 127.0.0.1 only, so nothing new listens on the network.
Settings › RemoteShared with phones lists the only Mac folders a phone can open. Each device gets three new permissions: Browse my files, Change my files, Terminal. Terminal is off for every device and asks once before turning on.
Menu barA static terminal glyph next to the Vitals icon while any phone has a terminal open. The panel's Using this Mac section lists sessions and browsing, with End, End All and Stop.
NotificationsOpened a terminal (End). Terminal is off for this phone (Settings). Got files (Show in Finder). No banner for browsing; it shows in the panel instead.
