Vitals Devices: user journeys

Plan v2.1, 2026-10-27. Built on your pick: device by device (direction B). Every device gets the same menu on every other device: Photos, Screenshots, Clipboard, Downloads, Documents, All files. The Mac is the hub that keeps all three connected. New things can also arrive on their own (direction C) as a setting. The v1 plan with the A/B/C comparison is here. Sample names in the screens are layout placeholders. Your decisions are in section 7.

8. Fixed today: a copied Mac screenshot didn't paste on the phone

What happened: you saved a screenshot on the Mac, then copied the file. The Mac recorded it as a file (no preview), and phones only paste text, links and images, so it never reached the phone's clipboard. A copy straight from a screenshot tool (like Shottr) was already an image and worked.

The fix: copying one image file (PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF) now records it as a picture with a preview. Phones can tap Copy, auto-copy picks it up, and Share › WhatsApp works. Several files at once stay files.
