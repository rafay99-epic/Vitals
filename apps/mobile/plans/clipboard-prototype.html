Vitals Remote · clipboard and drop · built on the A + B prototype

Clipboard, clickable.

Your Mac's clipboard history on the phone, and anything from the phone to the Mac: text, links, photos, files, folders. It adds one screen under Control and one share-sheet entry. Home, Apps, Alerts and Settings don't change. Everything in the phone works. Or run a journey on the right.
