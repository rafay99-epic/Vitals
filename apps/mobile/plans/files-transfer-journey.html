  • Tabs are the other devices. On the Pixel: Mac · iPhone. On the iPhone: Mac · Pixel. Only the Mac is connected? Just Mac.

  • Select, then Transfer. A sheet lists where it can go, never the device you're looking at. Copy only: the original stays.
