Vitals Remote · share · clipboard both ways + files both ways · built on the A + B prototype

Share, clickable.

One shared clipboard between your Mac and your phone, and LocalSend-style files in both directions: APKs, videos, photos, documents, whole folders. On the phone it is two tiles under Control, Clipboard and Files, plus Vitals in the share sheet. Home, Apps, Alerts and Settings don't change. Use the buttons under the phone to play the Mac's side, or run a journey. The Mac's own interactions are in the Mac prototype.
