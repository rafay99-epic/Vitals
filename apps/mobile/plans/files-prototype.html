Try it.

This is a working prototype, not a picture. Tap the tabs, the tiles, the images and the folders. Scroll inside the phone. Open a folder inside a folder.

Holding

  • Tabs are the other devices. Switch "Holding" to see them change.
  • Tap a file to preview it and Transfer it.
  • Select (top right) to pick several, then Transfer. The sheet never offers the device you're looking at.
  • Folders go as deep as the device does. Use the path under the title to jump back.
  • No clipboard here. Files only.

Names, sizes and pictures are placeholders.
